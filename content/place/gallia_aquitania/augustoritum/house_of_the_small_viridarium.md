## Garden Description
<!-- This is the main text describing the garden -->

Discovered in the course of a campaign of excavations in 1928, this house occupies part of insula VI-4 west of the forum.  It had a central courtyard [1] with a peristyle of columns of brick.  The garden, 11.5 meter long, occupied at least 90 square meters. Though incompletely explored, we know that it had in its center a vat for crushing lime and an area paved with reused marble plates with a molding.
